## Invoices

When billing is active, download PDF invoices from **Billing → Invoices** in the app. Statement descriptors usually include **Querify** or our legal entity name.

## Tax

Tax treatment depends on your country and entity type. If you need a VAT ID on the invoice, add it in billing settings or email [support@querify.ai](mailto:support@querify.ai).
