## Overview

Querify offers **Free**, **Starter**, **Pro**, and **Team** plans. Limits apply to queries per month, connection count, history retention, and seats. See the [Pricing](/pricing) page for current numbers.

## Changing plans

Upgrade or downgrade from **Billing** in your account when billing is enabled. Until billing is connected to your workspace, plan changes may be unavailable — contact [support@querify.ai](mailto:support@querify.ai) for enterprise or custom terms.
