## The short version

1. Connect a read-only database user.
2. Wait while Querify learns your schema (usually under a minute).
3. Ask a business question in the chat.
4. Read the summary, chart, and SQL on the result card.

That is the whole loop. Everything else — insights, exports, team settings — builds on top of this flow.
