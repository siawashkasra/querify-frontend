## What it means

The database user connected to Querify does not have `SELECT` on one or more objects your question needs.

## How to fix

Grant `SELECT` on the relevant schema or tables to your read-only user (see [read-only user](/help/database-setup/read-only-user)). If you use multiple schemas, repeat grants for each schema.

After changing grants, reconnect in Querify or run a new question.
