## Exports

Where available, download the tabular result as **CSV** or **Excel** from the result card. Exports reflect the row set returned for that answer, not your full database.

## Sharing

Share screenshots or exported files with your team. Do not paste credentials or connection strings into chat. For access control, use your workspace’s team and role settings when those features are enabled for your plan.
